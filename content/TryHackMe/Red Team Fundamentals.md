# Types of tests
- Vulnerability Assessment - identify as many vulnerabilities as possible, usu. automated scans and no exploitation
- Penetration Test - attempt to exploit vulnerabilities, conduct post-exploitation steps

# Red team engagements
- Shifts the focus from regular pentest to more on detecting and responding to real threat actor
- Red team emulates a real APT's TTPs to see what blue team can do 
- Clear goals can be defined, AKA obtaining flags or "crown jewels"
- Goal is not to find every vuln, but a single path that leads to the flags / crown jewels
- Can have red cell (attackers), blue cell (defenders), and white cell (referees)
